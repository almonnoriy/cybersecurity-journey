# Introduction to Cybersecurity

## 1. Overview

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computer systems, networks, applications and data against unauthorised access, damage, theft and disruption.

It involves technologies, security policies and procedures designed to protect digital environments and maintain the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information.

## 2. Areas Protected by Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity protects several important components of a digital environment.

### Networks
Communication infrastructure that connects computers, servers and other devices.

### Applications
Software and services used to perform digital operations and process information.

### Data
Information stored, processed or transmitted across digital systems.

### Endpoints
Devices such as computers, servers, laptops and mobile phones that connect to a network.

### Identities
Digital accounts and identities used to access systems and resources.

## 3. Importance of Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is important because it helps:

- Protect sensitive information.
- Prevent unauthorised system access.
- Reduce cybersecurity risks.
- Maintain the availability of digital services.
- Protect organisational infrastructure.
- Maintain trust in digital systems.

## 4. NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0

The NIST Cybersecurity Framework provides organisations with guidance for managing cybersecurity risks.

It is organised around six core functions:

1. **Govern:** Establish and oversee cybersecurity risk management strategy.
2. **Identify:** Understand assets, systems and cybersecurity risks.
3. **Protect:** Implement safeguards to manage identified risks.
4. **Detect:** Identify possible cybersecurity incidents.
5. **Respond:** Take action to address detected incidents.
6. **Recover:** Restore services and operations after incidents.

## 5. Information Assets in a University Environment

Examples of important university information assets include:

- Student academic records.
- Staff and administrative records.
- Financial information.
- Learning management systems.
- Network infrastructure.

These assets require appropriate protection to preserve their confidentiality, integrity and availability.
